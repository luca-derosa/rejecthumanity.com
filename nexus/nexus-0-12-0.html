Nexus 0.12.0: the ship fights back

by Luca De Rosa · 11 November 2026 · 2 min read

Nexus is the little tower-defence game I’m building on the side: a starship crashed on a medieval world, a maze of forest all around it, and monsters that are only ever born where you can’t see.

Until now the ship had no upgrades of its own. This update gives it two, one after the other.

Timber Shoring

Click the ship and you can shore it up for 80 wood. The hull goes from 100 to 200, and the ship’s four towers hit 50% harder and see 1 tile further. A fence of light now rings the site.

Lance Pylons

The second upgrade comes after the first, for 200 wood and 80 stone, so you need Stonework before you can buy it. A pylon rises on each corner of the site and fires a laser at the strongest monster in sight, up to 30 tiles away, one monster each. The beam burns harder the longer it holds, and it never reaches into the fog.

A cleaner crater

The crater reads cleaner on its east side: a hot rock that sat under a hull plate has moved off it.

Icons on every price

Every price and resource amount now shows its icon: the Nexus’s upgrades, a tower’s upgrade and tier prices, combo prices, plantations, the Transmuter, card details and the log.

Fixed

  • A far deposit’s beacon west of the ship could freeze the picture for a moment.
  • A tower or a Lure could be built on a rubble pile, and a monster climbing out of a mine or rising by a fallen star could land on one and stand there stuck.
  • A tower could pick up a drone on a tier that has none.
