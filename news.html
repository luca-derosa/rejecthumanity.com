First post: audiobook news and a new serial

Hey all! First post here.

I thought I’d give a quick update on where my books are at.

The Heralds of Change, book 2 of Bound to the Fallen, is just about done recording for the audiobook. It should launch very soon on Siren Audio.

Book 1, A Bond Between Equals, is also moving from Siren to Audible next week, if you were waiting for that :P

And I have a new serial on Royal Road, Supers Reforged. It posts free chapters there (minus the spicy chapters, which live on Patreon) and will move to Amazon when it’s done. The first chapters are a bit rough, but I’ll improve them, I swear!

Aaaand yeah. That’s pretty much it? Oh, I have a website now, and you can hit me up on my Discord server, so there’s that.
